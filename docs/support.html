Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my save or start over?

From the Dashboard, tap the account icon (top right) and choose "Switch Save." From the save picker you can delete any save slot with the trash icon, or create a new one in an empty slot.

I made a purchase but don't see it in the app.

Open the Store tab and tap "Restore Purchases." This re-applies any purchase already made on your Apple ID without charging you again. If it still doesn't appear, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll help sort it out.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your progress is stored locally on your device. We don't run our own servers and don't see your save data. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted with it, so be careful before uninstalling if you want to keep a save.

Why am I seeing ads, and can I remove them?

UltrasEuropa shows ads (via Google AdMob) to support development. The "Remove Ads" purchase in the Store removes every ad in the app, including the optional rewarded ad offer.
