Follow your club. Build your reputation.

UltrasEuropa is a football fan life simulator that puts you in the stands, not on the pitch. Create your character, choose your favorite club from real leagues across Europe, and work your way up from a regular fan to a respected Capo running the show.

What you'll do

Watch every match live

Minute-by-minute goals, cards, and reactions — react your way, for better or worse.

Climb the ranks

From Regular to Capo, growing your Loyalty, Knowledge, Influence, and Notoriety along the way.

Build a crew

Found your own youth group, chat with your crew, and make friends — or rivals — of other clubs' ultras.

A living season

A full generated calendar across 20 leagues and hundreds of real clubs, with tickets, standings, and form.
