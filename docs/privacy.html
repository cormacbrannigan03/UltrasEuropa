Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

UltrasEuropa ("the app") is developed by an independent developer (contact: cormac.brannigan03@gmail.com). This page explains what data the app and the third-party services it uses collect, and why.

Data we collect directly

UltrasEuropa doesn't have its own servers, accounts, or sign-in. Your character, stats, save slots, and progress are all stored locally on your device and are never sent to us. We have no way to see your save data, and deleting the app deletes it permanently.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

UltrasEuropa shows ads through Google's AdMob service to support development, and offers an optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase to turn them off entirely. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and app usage data, and may request permission to use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for more relevant ads — you can decline this at any time without losing access to the app. Where required (e.g. in the EU/UK), you'll be shown a consent prompt before any ad loads, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform.

For details on what Google collects and how it's used, see Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure.

Purchases (Apple)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details — Apple's own privacy policy covers that part of the transaction.

Children's privacy

UltrasEuropa is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Ads are not configured to be treated as child-directed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cormac.brannigan03@gmail.com.
